Problem Statement 2 – Output Guidelines Package: MC011A – PTCA (Percutaneous Transluminal Coronary Angioplasty) Purpose of the Outputs Participants must generate two outputs for each claim: S. No. Output 1 Structured, row-wise output capturing:  • Objective interpretation of pre-procedure and post-procedure angiographic images  • Correlation with written angiography report  • Alignment with PTCA STG requirements  • Stage and timeline awareness 2 Textual summary that consolidates all structured outputs into a human-readable note suitable for PPD / CPD review, without making diagnostic, approval, or payment decisions  !"#$ Note: For PTCA, both pre-procedure and post-procedure angiograms are mandatory.    
  
SECTION 1: Claim Details (1 column) Claim No. • Description: Unique identiﬁer for the claim • Type: String / Integer • Purpose: Links angiographic interpretation to a speciﬁc PTCA claim  SECTION 2: Image Classiﬁcation (2 columns) 1. Type of Radiological Image – Pre-Procedure Stage • Description: Free text, Imaging submitted at pre-authorization / diagnostic stage • Examples:  o “Cardiac angiogram (single still)” o “Cardiac angiogram (multiple stills)” o “Absent” • Purpose: Conﬁrms diagnostic angiogram availability as per PTCA STG  2. Type of Radiological Image – Post-Procedure Stage • Description: Imaging submitted at claim stage after PTCA • Expected Values:  o “Cardiac angiogram (single still)” o “Cardiac angiogram (multiple stills)” o “Absent” • Purpose: Conﬁrms post-procedure evidence submission (mandatory in PTCA)  SECTION 3: Image Description – Coronary Anatomy (9 columns) %& Describe what is seen in each vessel, not what it means These ﬁelds capture objective, observable image features described under following nine mandatory headings/ columns 3A. Pre-Procedure Image Description (4 columns, pipe separated sub-categories) -  1. LMCA (Left Main Coronary Artery)  § Proximal  § Distal 2. LAD (Left Anterior Descending artery)  § Proximal  § Mid  § Distal  3. LCX (Left Circumﬂex Artery)  § Proximal  
§ Distal  § OM 4. RCA (Right Coronary Artery) § Proximal  § Mid  § Distal  • Expected Content (per artery): free-text, observational description such as -   o “Proximal – no disease” o “Mid – 40–50% luminal narrowing/ stenosis” o “Distal – not visualised”  3B. Post-Procedure Image Description (5 columns, pipe separated sub-categories) -  1. LMCA (Left Main Coronary Artery) § Proximal  § Distal 2. LAD (Left Anterior Descending artery)  § Proximal  § Mid  § Distal  3. LCX (Left Circumﬂex Artery)  § Proximal  § Distal  § OM 4. RCA (Right Coronary Artery) § Proximal  § Mid  § Distal  • Expected Content (per artery): free-text, observational description such as -   o “Flow restored” o “No residual narrowing seen” o “Not visualised” 5. Stent Visualization - Yes or no  SECTION 4: Report Validity and Correlation (for both pre-procedure stage and post-procedure stage in this package) (6 columns) 4A. Pre-Procedure Report Validity  1. Intra-Report Consistency Check: Whether the written report’s observations and impression agree with each other; expected values – 
o Consistent o Partially supported o Unsupported 2. Reason (intra): Free text (e.g. “Observations and impression match”)  3. Image vs Written Report Correlation (Inter): Whether image features support what the written report claims; expected values – o Consistent o Partially supported o Unsupported 4. Reason (inter): Free text explaining mismatch or support  4B. Post-procedure Report Validity (only inter-report validity in post-procedure stage required for this package) 5. Image vs Written Report Correlation (Inter): Whether image features support what the written report claims; expected values – o Consistent o Partially supported o Unsupported 6. Reason (inter): Free text explaining mismatch or support  SECTION 5: Claim, Package & STG Alignment (overall) (4 columns) 5A. Claim and Package Alignment  1. Alignment Category: Expected values -  o Both pre-procedure and post-procedure investigations align o Only pre-procedure investigations align o Only post-procedure investigations align o None of the investigations align 2. Reason for Alignment / Non-Alignment: Free text (e.g. “Post-procedure angiogram mandatory as per STG”)  5B. Investigation and STG Alignment  3. STG Alignment Outcome: Expected values -  o Consistent o Partially supported o Unsupported 4. Reason for Deviation (mapped to PPD / CPD logic): Free text (e.g. “Flow obstruction as per STGs do not require procedure”)

Problem Statement 2 – Output Guidelines Package: MG029A – Acute Exacerbation of COPD Purpose of the Outputs Participants must generate two outputs for each claim: S. No.  Output 1 Structured, row-wise output that captures:  • Objective interpretation of the radiological image (Chest X-ray) • Correlation with written radiology report • Alignment with STG expectations • Stage and timeline awareness • Review-ready ﬂags 2 Textual summary capturing all details of the structured output in a human readable format suitable for PPD / CPD review and must not contain diagnostic, approval, or payment decisions  !"#$ Note: For Acute exacerbation of COPD, only post-procedure imaging is expected. No re-procedure radiological image is mandated.

Lung Fields: free text, Overall appearance of lung parenchyma o Examples of features noted may include:  § Clear § Hyperinﬂated § Patchy opacities § Basal opacities § Not clear (poor inspiration / artefacts)  2. Left and Right Costophrenic (CP) Angles: free text; Appearance of CP angles o Examples of features noted may include:  § Sharp § Blunted (unilateral / bilateral) § Not assessable  3. Left and Right Hilum: free text; Appearance of hilar shadows o Examples of features noted may include:  § Normal § Prominent 
§ Not well visualised  4. Midline Shift: free text; Position of trachea / mediastinum o Examples of features noted may include:  § Not seen § Slight left / right shift  5. Cardiac Size: free text; Visual impression of cardiac silhouette o Examples of features noted may include:   § Normal § Enlarged § Appears small / narrow   SECTION 4: Report Validity and Correlation (only for post-procedure stage in this package) (4 columns) 1. Intra-Report Consistency Check: Whether the written report’s observations and impression agree with each other; expected values:  o Consistent o Partially supported o Unsupported 2. Reasoning: free text, examples may include:  o “Observations match impression” o “Impression given without supporting observations”  3. Image vs Written Report Correlation: Whether image features support what the written report claims; expected values:  o Consistent o Partially supported o Unsupported 4. Reasoning: free text, examples may include:  o “X-ray features support reported ﬁndings” o “Image does not show features claimed in report”  SECTION 5: Claim, Package & STG Alignment (overall) (4 columns) 
5A. Claim and Package Alignment: two columns to be ﬁlled -  1. Alignment Category: Expected values -  o Both pre-procedure and post-procedure investigations align o Only pre-procedure investigations align o Only post-procedure investigations align o None of the investigations align  2. Reasoning for alignment/ partial alignment/ nonalignment: free text, examples may include:  o “Only post-procedure image is required as per STG” o “Pre-procedure X-ray submitted but STG expects post-procedure image”  5B. Investigation and STG Alignment: two columns to be ﬁlled -  3. STG Alignment Outcome: Expected values -  o Consistent o Partially supported o Unsupported  4. Reasoning for alignment/ partial alignment/ nonalignment: Textual explanation mapped to PPD / CPD logic of STGs; examples may include:  o “Hyperinﬂation seen; other features of acute exacerbation not evident” o “Image quality limits assessment” SECTION 6: Stage & Timeline Awareness (5 columns) S. No. Column Description 1 Admission Date DD/MM/YYYY 2 Pre-Procedure Investigation Date DD/MM/YYYY 3 Post-Procedure Investigation Date DD/MM/YYYY 4 Discharge Date DD/MM/YYYY 5 Flag before admission date or after discharge date documents for post-procedure investigations Yes / No   SECTION 7: Quality (1 column) • Description: free text, image usability comment, examples may include:  o Good enough for human eyes o Poor quality, artefacts present
